The 4R Framework for UN SDG 12

1. Refuse & Reduce

Avoid single-use plastic bags, disposable cutlery, and over-packaged goods at source.

2. Reuse & Repurpose

Extend product life through repair, donation, glass container refilling, and creative upcycling.

3. Clean Recycling

Empty and rinse food containers. Wet or greasy materials contaminate whole recycling bales.

4. Rot / Compost

Divert vegetable rinds and fruit peels to compost to produce organic fertilizer for soil.
