Academic Project Disclosure & Transparency Policy

EcoSort AI is currently implemented as an internship prototype aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 12. Current EcoPoints are purely virtual gamification tokens and carry no monetary cash redemption value at this stage.

  • No municipal partnership or Nagar Palika agreement has been executed yet. Any future program will be announced after independent civic agreements are finalized.
  • EcoSort AI does not promise monetary compensation, cash payouts, or guaranteed merchant vouchers.
  • We strictly refrain from creating fake partner logos, fabricated coupon codes, or simulated redemption transactions.

Proposed Phase 2 Partnership Architecture

1. Local Eco-Vendors (Proposed)

Partnering with local bulk-grocers, farmers' markets, and zero-plastic cafes to offer small percentage discounts in exchange for verified EcoPoint milestones.

2. Civic Recognition (Proposed)

Issuing ward-level certificates of environmental stewardship and honoring top community households during municipal cleanliness drives (Swachhata campaigns).

3. Community Green Projects (Proposed)

Pooling community EcoPoints toward neighborhood urban tree planting, public composting units, and school environmental education drives.

Technical Extensibility for Evaluators

The backend database schema incorporates an immutable RewardTransaction ledger with strict idempotency keys. When civic or commercial partnerships are established, a partner redemption service can plug directly into the existing ledger without refactoring core business logic.
