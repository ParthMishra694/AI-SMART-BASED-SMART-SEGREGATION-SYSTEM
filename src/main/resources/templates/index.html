UN SDG 12 Commitment

Responsible Consumption and Production

Sustainable Development Goal 12 aims to ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns. Municipal solid waste is growing at unprecedented rates; source segregation is the single most effective intervention preventing recyclable materials from degrading in anaerobic landfills.

  • Target 12.3: Reduce per-capita household food waste through organic segregation and composting guidance.
  • Target 12.5: Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling, and reuse (the 4 Rs).
